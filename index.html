    ✅ Safer Steps

      🚩 Red Flags

      Be cautious of upfront fees, pressure tactics, unclear terms, and requests for OTPs or PINs.

      🟢 Safer Borrowing

      Verify the lender independently, read all terms, and keep copies of documents and communications.

      🛡️ Before You Accept a Loan

      • Verify the lender using official regulator or bank sources.
      • Read the complete loan agreement and all applicable fees.
      • Never share an OTP, PIN, or banking password.
      • Do not pay suspicious advance fees to release a loan.
      • Contact your bank or local cybercrime authorities if you suspect fraud.
