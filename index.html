      🛡️ Before You Accept a Loan

      • Verify the lender through official sources.
      • Read the full agreement, APR and all fees.
      • Never share OTPs, PINs or banking passwords.
      • Do not pay suspicious advance fees to release a loan.
      • Report suspected fraud to your bank and relevant authorities.
